Follow Back Checker

Drop the zip Instagram gives you when you download your information. See who you follow that doesn’t follow you back, who follows you without a follow back, and who follows you both ways.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your files are read here and never uploaded.

1. Ask Instagram for your lists

  1. In the Instagram app or on the web, open Settings, then Accounts Center.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions, then Download your information.
  3. Choose Download or transfer information, pick your Instagram profile, then Some of your information.
  4. Tick Followers and following only, then Download to device.
  5. Set Date range to All time and Format to JSON, then Create files.
  6. Instagram emails you when the zip is ready. It usually takes minutes, sometimes up to two days.

Menu names move around a little between app versions. Any export that contains followers_1.json and following.json works, and so does the HTML format.

2. Drop the export here

The .zip itself, or the files inside it: followers_1.json and following.json.

    Usernames from your following list are matched against your followers list, lowercased. Dates are the timestamps Instagram records for each follow, so they are only as complete as the export.

    The last export you drop stays in this browser only, so you can come back and keep ticking people off. Clear removes it. Ticks are kept per list and per username.
