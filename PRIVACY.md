# Privacy

Follow Back Checker runs entirely inside your own browser session on instagram.com, or inside the Android app's own browser window.

- It never asks for your Instagram password. You log in on Instagram's own page.
- It sends requests only to instagram.com: the same requests the website makes when you scroll a followers list, plus the unfollow request the website makes when you tap Unfollow, which this tool only sends for one account at a time after you confirm it.
- Your follower lists, history and settings are stored only on your device (extension storage, the site's local storage, or the app's private storage).
- Nothing is sent to the author or to any third party. There is no server, no analytics and no crash reporting.
- The Android app downloads the latest checker script from this repository over HTTPS so fixes reach you without reinstalling. That is the only request it makes outside instagram.com.
- Uninstalling the extension or the app deletes everything it stored.

This project is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
